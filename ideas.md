# WorkerGigBD - Design Brainstorm

## Brand Identity
- **Name**: WorkerGigBD
- **Domain**: workergigbd.com
- **Tagline**: "Find Work. Earn Money. Grow Skills."
- **Style**: Professional corporate — clean, trustworthy, modern
- **All content in English**

## Color Palette
- **Primary**: Deep Blue (#1E3A5F) — trust, professionalism
- **Accent**: Emerald Green (#059669) — money, growth, success
- **Background**: Off-white (#F8FAFC)
- **Card**: White (#FFFFFF) with subtle shadow
- **Text**: Slate-900 (#0F172A) headings, Slate-600 (#475569) body
- **Danger**: Rose-500
- **Warning**: Amber-500

## Typography
- Headings: Plus Jakarta Sans (700/800)
- Body: Inter (400/500)
- Monospace: JetBrains Mono (for numbers/IDs)

## Layout Structure
- **Landing**: Navbar, Hero, Features, How It Works, Stats, CTA, Footer
- **Dashboard**: Left sidebar + topbar + content area
- **Admin Panel**: Sidebar with Users, Jobs, Withdrawals, Notifications, Logs

## Key Requirements
- No fake data — empty states only
- Database placeholders ready
- SEO: robots.txt, privacy, terms, about
- Admin panel with full management
- Real-time notifications
